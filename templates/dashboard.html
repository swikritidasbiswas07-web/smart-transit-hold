How Smart Transit Hold Works

When a suspicious call is detected close to a risky payment attempt, the system does not instantly complete the transfer. Instead, it places the payment under Smart Transit Hold and asks the customer to verify independently.

Phone Call → Suspect Spam/Fraud → Audio Analysis → Transfer Attempt → Risk Fusion → Smart Transit Hold
