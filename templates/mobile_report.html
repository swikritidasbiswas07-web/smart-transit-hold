Phone-Side Scam Reporting Flow

This screen simulates the real mobile banking app workflow. In production, the customer would tap Suspect Spam/Fraud during or after a suspicious phone call. The call audio or transcript would then be securely sent to the bank backend for analysis.

Prototype note: this page does not record live phone calls. It simulates the phone-side button by allowing you to upload a saved call recording such as MP3, WAV, M4A, or similar audio file.
